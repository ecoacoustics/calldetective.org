Giving Nature a Voice

In a critical biodiversity crisis with over a million species threatened, acoustic data offers key insights into ecosystem health. To address this, Google DeepMind developed Perch, an AI tool for analyzing audio to identify and monitor species.

Call Detective, developed by Open Ecoacoustics together with Google's Digital First Initiative, lets citizen-experts validate bird calls from large acoustic datasets to build precise, localized classifiers. Together, these efforts transform passive listening into proactive conservation, helping protect habitats and fauna.
